# Nimbus Analytics — Support and Service Level Agreement

## Support channels

| Plan       | Channels                               | Hours                    |
|------------|----------------------------------------|--------------------------|
| Starter    | Community forum and documentation      | —                        |
| Business   | Email and in-app chat                  | Monday–Friday, 08:00–20:00 CET |
| Enterprise | Email, chat, phone and a dedicated Slack channel | 24/7           |

Enterprise customers are also assigned a named Customer Success Manager.

## Response time targets

| Severity | Description                                    | Business  | Enterprise |
|----------|------------------------------------------------|-----------|------------|
| P1       | Platform down or data loss                     | 4 hours   | 1 hour     |
| P2       | Major feature broken, no workaround            | 8 hours   | 4 hours    |
| P3       | Minor issue or workaround exists               | 2 business days | 1 business day |
| P4       | Question or feature request                    | 5 business days | 2 business days |

Response time is measured from when the ticket is opened to the first response from a human
support engineer. Automated replies do not count as a response.

## Uptime commitment

Nimbus commits to 99.9% monthly uptime on the Business plan and 99.95% on the Enterprise plan.
The Starter plan has no uptime commitment. Scheduled maintenance is announced at least 7 days in
advance, happens on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC, and does not count as downtime.

## Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, the customer can claim a service credit:

- Below the commitment but at least 99.0%: 10% of the monthly fee.
- Below 99.0% but at least 95.0%: 25% of the monthly fee.
- Below 95.0%: 50% of the monthly fee.

Credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the affected month and are applied to the
next invoice. Service credits are the customer's only remedy for missed uptime and cannot be
exchanged for cash.
