# Nimbus Analytics — Product and Pricing Guide

Nimbus Analytics is a cloud platform for collecting, transforming and visualising business data.
It was launched in 2021 and is operated by Nimbus Labs Ltd., headquartered in Dublin, Ireland.

## Core features

- **Connectors**: more than 140 managed connectors, including PostgreSQL, Snowflake, Salesforce,
  HubSpot, Stripe and Google Sheets. New connectors are released every two weeks.
- **Pipelines**: visual and SQL-based transformation pipelines. Pipelines can be scheduled as often
  as every 5 minutes on the Business plan and every 1 minute on the Enterprise plan.
- **Dashboards**: drag-and-drop dashboards with 25 chart types, scheduled PDF exports and
  embeddable public links.
- **Nimbus Assist**: a natural-language assistant that turns plain-English questions into SQL.
  Nimbus Assist is available on the Business and Enterprise plans only.

## Plans

| Plan       | Price                         | Users included | Monthly data rows |
|------------|-------------------------------|----------------|-------------------|
| Starter    | Free                          | 3              | 1 million         |
| Business   | $49 per user per month        | 5 minimum      | 50 million        |
| Enterprise | Custom pricing (annual only)  | 25 minimum     | Unlimited         |

Annual billing on the Business plan gives a 20% discount compared with monthly billing.
Additional data rows on the Business plan cost $10 per 10 million rows.

## Trials

Every new workspace starts with a 14-day free trial of the Business plan. No credit card is
required to start a trial. When the trial ends, the workspace is moved to the Starter plan
automatically unless a paid plan is selected.

## Refunds and cancellation

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current
billing period and monthly payments are not refundable. Annual plans can be refunded in full
within 30 days of the initial purchase. After 30 days, annual plans are not refundable, but the
plan stays active until the end of the paid term.

## Data limits and storage

Starter workspaces keep query history for 30 days. Business workspaces keep query history for
1 year, and Enterprise workspaces keep it for as long as the contract is active. Uploaded files
are limited to 2 GB each on all plans.
